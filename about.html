About Us

Remembering the Shuhada, and the love that shaped how they lived.

Idara Sirat ul Ishq is a publishing house and bookstore dedicated to the lives of the Shuhada. We bring their stories to Urdu readers through biographies, memories and accounts of faith and sacrifice, including the Shuhada who defended the holy shrines (Madafean-e-Haram) and those devoted to the Ahl al-Bayt (a.s.).

We believe a martyr is shaped by a lifetime of prayer, sincerity and love, and not only by the moment of martyrdom. That is why our books show how the Shuhada lived: their character, their relationships and their devotion to Hazrat Fatima Zahra (s.a.) and to the path of truth.

Every book is chosen to inspire readers, young and old, to know these heroes and carry their example into their own lives.
